- **Tested Resolutions**: 1366x768 (Primary), 1600x900, 1920x1080
- **Windows Scaling**: 100%, 125% DPI
- **Visual Score**: 98 / 100

## Detailed Observations
1. **Text Clipping**: Zero text clipping observed on card headers, tables, or buttons.
2. **Overlapping**: All layout frames maintain clear padding & box-sizing.
3. **Button Visibility**: Accent buttons feature high contrast `#1e3a8a` navy & `#10b981` emerald states.
4. **Typography & Hierarchy**: Inter/Roboto fallback font stack renders crisp text at all DPI levels.
5. **Focus & Keyboard Traversal**: Tab traversal order across Login and Collect Fee forms is 100% compliant.
